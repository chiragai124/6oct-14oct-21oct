Activity dropped in the second week
Messages fell from 2,483 to 601 (−76%) across all communities. Part of week 1 is the 14 Oct spike in Community #4's General group (663 messages in one day), but every community was quieter in week 2.

Posted by the amber Team

  • 8 Oct: In Community #2's UK group, the team answered a London accommodation cost question and pointed to amberstudent.com.
  • 9 Oct: Webinar topic survey sent to all communities, asking what live webinars hosted by international students should cover. A reminder with the form link followed on 12 Oct.
  • 16 Oct: Scholarship Application Links page shared in the General groups.
  • 17 Oct: Scam warning in Community #4 after a suspicious link was deleted, telling members not to click unknown links.

Headline Takeaways

  • Community #4 is the whole community right now. It had 2,781 of 3,084 messages (90%) and 309 of 410 active members. The busiest day was 14 Oct, with 663 messages in its General group alone.
  • "How do I apply for Amber Scholar?" is the most repeated question. Members kept asking about CGPA, IELTS, acceptance letters, professor acceptance, applying after Class 12 and transfer students. A pinned official FAQ would answer most of these.
  • Trust questions about Amber Scholar came up in two communities. In #1 Australia, members asked whether anyone had actually received the scholarship and whether their data was just being collected. In #4 General, members questioned whether the group is linked to the official amber site and noted the admins were silent.
  • Communities #1 and #2 are close to silent. They had 47 and 65 messages, and #1's Germany group had none.
  • Good organic moments in #3. Members shared new offer letters (Anglia Ruskin, Hofstra, a US R1 university) and the group celebrated together.
Needs Attention
  • Incorrect claims about Amber Scholar (#4): one member posted unverified claims about the scholarship amount and who is eligible, and repeated them across several groups. An official correction or pinned FAQ is worth posting.
  • Unsolicited posts: a legal help request copied into 4 groups (#2); a "DM me" message copied into 4 groups (#3); a private room-to-rent listing (#1 UK); a member offering to "help with your application" (#4); unrelated promo links (#3 UK, #4 USA).
